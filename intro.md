# Finicky Technologies Ltd

We are a software service company offering website & web application development, as well as custom software solutions for individuals, businesses, organisations and government institutions alike.

---

## Who We Are

Finicky Technologies Ltd's current focus is on building websites, web applications and other technological solutions properly with the kind of care and attention to detail that gets things right the first time. Before turning to software engineering, the founder trained and worked as a lawyer. This background influences how work gets done - carefully, thoroughly, and built to hold up under scrutiny.

## What We Do

We build **websites** that not only embodies your organisation, but also works smoothly for the people using it, whether its your customers, your staff, or the public.

We also build **web applications** which are custom software that businesses and institutions rely on to run specific parts of their operations online, whether that's managing payments & invoicing, keeping records, verifying users, or handling day-to-day administrative work.

### 3. AMAC SmartTax (2025-2026 - Nigeria)

![AMAC SmartTax landing page hero section](https://res.cloudinary.com/kaygeea-tech/image/upload/v1785975563/smarttax_fe_hero_wgghuo.png "SmartTax hero section")
